This Privacy Policy explains how 3DWebSoft IT Solutions ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information through the Future Tech School Program website, admissions system, Super Admin dashboard, School Coordinator portal, and Student portal (together, the "Program"). We know that a meaningful share of this information relates to children enrolled through their school or parent/guardian, and we handle it accordingly — see Children's Privacy below.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the Program, we may collect:

  • Student information: name, date of birth, grade, program level, and the school they are associated with.
  • Parent/guardian information: name, mobile number, and contact details provided during an individual admission.
  • School information: school name, coordinator contact details, and enrolment/enquiry records, for schools onboarded by our Super Admin.
  • Payment records: amount paid, payment status, and our own payment reference number. Payments are made by scanning a UPI QR code and paying directly into our bank account — we do not receive or store your UPI PIN, full bank account details, or card numbers; those never pass through our systems at all.
  • Account & login data: email/username and a securely hashed password (we never store passwords in plain text) for Super Admin, School Coordinator, and Student accounts.
  • Technical data: basic request logs (e.g. IP address, timestamp, page requested) collected automatically for security, rate-limiting, and abuse prevention.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process admissions, enquiries, and payments, and to generate receipts;
  • To create and manage Super Admin, School Coordinator, and Student accounts;
  • To show each School Coordinator only their own school's students and enquiries;
  • To respond to contact-form messages and support requests;
  • To secure the Program against fraud, abuse, and unauthorised access (for example, rate-limiting login and payment attempts);
  • To meet legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations connected with payments we process.

3. Payments

Payments are made directly to our bank account by scanning a UPI QR code with your own UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, or your bank's app) — there is no third-party payment gateway involved, and no payment credentials of yours ever pass through our systems. Because of this, payments are confirmed manually: a member of our team checks the bank account for a matching transaction (using the payment reference shown to you at the time of payment) before marking it paid and issuing a receipt. This usually happens within a few minutes but is not instantaneous.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only:

  • With the relevant School Coordinator, limited strictly to students and records belonging to their own school;
  • With our bank/UPI provider, solely as an unavoidable consequence of you paying us directly through UPI;
  • With service providers who host our servers, database, or (in future) send transactional emails on our behalf, under obligations to protect the data;
  • Where required to comply with a legal obligation, court order, or valid request from a public authority.

5. Data Retention

We retain admission, payment, and account records for as long as the related school or student relationship is active, and afterwards for as long as needed to meet accounting, legal, or dispute-resolution obligations. You can contact us to ask about or request deletion of specific records, subject to those obligations.

6. How We Protect Your Data

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored or displayed in plain text after initial creation;
  • Sessions use signed, expiring JSON Web Tokens, issued separately for admin, school, and student accounts;
  • Fees charged are always calculated on the server, never trusted from the browser;
  • The API applies security headers, rate limiting on login/payment/contact endpoints, and restricts which website origins may call it;
  • A school account can be suspended immediately, blocking further access, without deleting its underlying data, in case of a security concern.

No system can be guaranteed 100% secure, but we work to apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the information we hold.

7. School & Student Data Isolation

Each School Coordinator account can only see enquiries, students, and payment records tied to their own school. A student's own portal shows only that student's profile information. Only the Super Admin has visibility across all schools.

8. Children's Privacy

The Program is designed for use by schools (grades 5–12) and by parents or guardians enrolling a child. Student accounts can only be created after a parent- or school-initiated, paid admission, and are intended to be used by the student under a parent, guardian, or school's supervision. We collect only the student information reasonably necessary for admission, coursework, and safeguarding data isolation described above. Parents or guardians who wish to review, correct, or request deletion of their child's information may contact us at any time using the details below.

9. Your Rights & Choices

  • You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you or your child;
  • You may request correction of inaccurate information;
  • You may request deletion of information we no longer need to retain for legal or accounting reasons;
  • School Coordinators and Students can change their own account password at any time from their respective portal.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 12 below.

10. Cookies

The website uses only the minimal storage needed to keep you signed in to your admin, school, or student portal session. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Program or applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
