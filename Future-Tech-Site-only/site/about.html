About Future Tech

School Program

Building Future Innovators Today!

The Future Tech School Program is a technology-focused
education initiative designed to prepare students for a
rapidly changing digital world.

Launched by 3DWebSoft Foundation and developed by the
technology expertise of 3DWebSoft IT Solutions, the program
aims to introduce students to emerging technologies at an
early stage and help them develop the skills, creativity and
confidence needed to become tomorrow's innovators.

From 5th to 12th Standard, students progress through a
structured, level-based leaming journey covering Computer
Science, Coding & Programming, Artificial Intelligence,
Machine Learning, Cybersecurity, Robotics, Generative Al,
Data Science and Innovation.

Our approach goes beyond traditional classroom learning.
Students are encouraged to learn concepts, practice their
skills, build projects, present their ideas and continuously grow.

We believe students learn technology best when they can experiment with it and apply their knowledge to meaningful
problems. Through hands-on activities, practical projects, workshops, mentorship and technology-based challenges,
we create opportunities for students to become active creators rather than passive users of technology.

Our Mission

To make future-ready technology education accessible,
practical and engaging for students, while developing the
technical and personal skills they need to confidently
navigate the future.

We aim to help students:

Build strong digital and programming foundations

Explore emerging technologies at an appropriate level

Develop logical and problem-solving abilities

Encourage creativity, innovation and curiosity

Gain hands-on experience through projects

Develop teamwork and communication skills

Understand responsible and ethical use of technology

Discover different technology and career pathways

Our Vision

To empower the next generation to learn, create and
innovate with technology.

We envision a future where students don't simply consume
technology-they understand it, build with it and use it
responsibly to solve real-world problems.

Why Future Tech School Program?

Technology is evolving rapidly. Artificial Intelligence, automation, cybersecurity, data science and other emerging technologies
are becoming an increasingly important part of our world.

The Future Tech School Program gives students an opportunity to start understanding these technologies step by step, at the
right stage of their education.

Our Four-Level Learning Journey
Level 1
Junior Innovations

5th-6th Standard Building digital foundations through computer basics, Al awareness, Scratch programming, cyber safety and beginner projects.
Level 2
Young Technologies

7th-8th Standard Introducing Python, Al tools,machine learning fundamentals, cybersecurity and Al-based projects.
Level 3
Future
Engineers

9th-10th Standard Developing deeper skills in Python, machine learning, data science, ethica cybersecurity and real-world projects.
Level 4
AI
Professional

11th-12th Standard Exploring advanced Python, AI/ML, Generative Al, cybersecurity and capstone project development.
Our Commitment

We are committed to creating a safe, engaging and practical learning environment
where every student gets the opportunity to explore technology, ask questions,
experiment with ideas and develop confidence.

Our goal is not simply to teach students a particular programming language or technology.

Our goal is to develop future-ready learners, problem solvers and innovators.
