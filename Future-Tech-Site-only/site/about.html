About Us

About Future Tech School Program

Building Future Innovators Today!

The Future Tech School Program is a technology-focused education initiative designed to prepare students for a rapidly changing digital world.

Launched by 3DWebSoft Foundation and developed by the technology expertise of 3DWebSoft IT Solutions, the program aims to introduce students to emerging technologies at an early stage and help them develop the skills, creativity and confidence needed to become tomorrow's innovators.

From 5th to 12th Standard, students progress through a structured, level-based learning journey covering:

Computer ScienceCoding & ProgrammingArtificial IntelligenceMachine LearningCybersecurityRoboticsGenerative AIData ScienceInnovation

Our Learning Philosophy

Our approach goes beyond traditional classroom learning. Students are encouraged to learn concepts, practice their skills, build projects, present their ideas and continuously grow.

LEARN
PRACTICE
BUILD
PRESENT
GROW

We believe students learn technology best when they can experiment with it and apply their knowledge to meaningful problems. Through hands-on activities, practical projects, workshops, mentorship and technology-based challenges, we create opportunities for students to become active creators rather than passive users of technology.

Our Mission

To make future-ready technology education accessible, practical and engaging for students, while developing the technical and personal skills they need to confidently navigate the future.

We aim to help students:

  • Build strong digital and programming foundations
  • Explore emerging technologies at an appropriate level
  • Develop logical and problem-solving abilities
  • Encourage creativity, innovation and curiosity
  • Gain hands-on experience through projects
  • Develop teamwork and communication skills
  • Understand responsible and ethical use of technology
  • Discover different technology and career pathways

Our Vision

To empower the next generation to learn, create and innovate with technology.

We envision a future where students don't simply consume technology — they understand it, build with it and use it responsibly to solve real-world problems.

Why Future Tech School Program?

Technology is evolving rapidly. Artificial Intelligence, automation, cybersecurity, data science and other emerging technologies are becoming an increasingly important part of our world. The Future Tech School Program gives students an opportunity to start understanding these technologies step by step, at the right stage of their education.

Conceptual LearningUnderstand the fundamentals.
Hands-on PracticeLearn by doing.
Real-World ProjectsTurn ideas into working projects.
InnovationThink creatively and explore new possibilities.
PresentationLearn to communicate and demonstrate ideas.
Future ReadinessBuild skills for further education.

Our Four-Level Learning Journey

A structured path from Grade 5th to 12th, with each level building on the last.

LEVEL 1

Junior Innovators

5th–6th Standard

Building digital foundations through computer basics, AI awareness, Scratch programming, cyber safety and beginner projects.

View curriculum
LEVEL 2

Young Technologists

7th–8th Standard

Introducing Python, AI tools, machine learning fundamentals, cybersecurity and AI-based projects.

View curriculum
LEVEL 3

Future Engineers

9th–10th Standard

Developing deeper skills in Python, machine learning, data science, ethical hacking, cybersecurity and real-world projects.

View curriculum
LEVEL 4

AI Professional Foundation

11th–12th Standard

Exploring advanced Python, AI/ML, Generative AI, cybersecurity and capstone project development.

View curriculum

Our Commitment

We are committed to creating a safe, engaging and practical learning environment where every student gets the opportunity to explore technology, ask questions, experiment with ideas and develop confidence.

Our goal is not simply to teach students a particular programming language or technology.

Our goal is to develop future-ready learners, problem solvers and innovators.
